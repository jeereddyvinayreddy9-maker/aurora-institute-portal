About Aurora

Founded to make quality technical education reachable, Aurora combines strong academics with clubs, sports and community service. Our goal is simple: every student leaves able to build, lead and care.

Departments

Computer Science & Engineering

  • Industry-style coding labs
  • Hackathons and coding club
  • Internships from year 2

Electronics & Communication

For parents

Choosing a college is a family decision. Here is what parents ask us most.

🛡️Safe campus

24×7 security, CCTV on all main routes and ID-based entry at every gate.

🏡Hostel and transport

Separate hostels with resident wardens, hygienic mess and bus routes across the city.

💳Fees and scholarships

Fee details are shared during counselling. Merit and need-based scholarships are available.

📊Track your ward

Sign in as a Parent to follow attendance and announcements.

🧑‍🏫Mentoring and wellbeing

Every student gets a faculty mentor, and a counsellor is on campus on weekdays.

💼Careers

Aptitude and interview training starts in the first year.

Common questions

How do I know how my child is doing?

Sign in as a Parent to view attendance. Mentors also call parents each semester.

Is the hostel safe for girls?

Yes. The girls’ hostel has its own gate, women wardens and round-the-clock security.

Can we visit the campus?

Yes. Use the callback form and the office will arrange a campus visit.

What if my child needs help with stress?

The on-campus counsellor and the faculty mentor are the first contacts. Parents are informed when needed.

Leave your details and our office will call you.
